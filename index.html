Feature Deep Dive

Explore the Full PriceLister Suite

Comprehensive overview of every module powering your everyday commerce.

1. Product Master & Item Directory

Maintain an accurate product master database. Every product record includes:

  • Product name, internal code, and description
  • Size / weight and unit of measure
  • Multi-level category classification
  • UPC / barcode with camera scanning
  • Cost price, base price, selling price, and retail MRP
  • Real-time stock quantity and reorder threshold level
  • Multiple high-resolution product photos & variation attributes

2. Smart Dynamic Price Lister

Generate clean, professional price catalogs filtered by category, client group, or date. Export formatted Excel spreadsheets or print layouts suitable for wholesale clients or store displays.

3. Customer & Client Relations

Maintain a complete customer directory with contact information, purchase history, and client notes. Link invoices and price catalogs directly to individual clients.

4. Retail & B2B Invoicing Suite

Dual invoicing engines tailored for everyday sales:

  • Customer Invoices: Rapid POS counter billing with thermal slip printing.
  • Business Invoices: B2B bulk orders with company tax info, custom branding, and 4 designer red templates.

5. Standalone Customer Storefront

Dedicated customer-facing catalog portal where clients can browse inventory, calculate cart totals, and submit WhatsApp orders with 1 tap.

6. Multi-Tenant Role Security

Three built-in roles protect your data integrity:

  • Creator Admin: Full ownership, billing, delete authority, and worker management.
  • Co-Admin: Day-to-day operations, products, and invoices.
  • Worker: Task-focused execution with restricted access to cost and deletion.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At PriceLister we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard information when you use our service.

1. Data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • Identity data – name associated with your Google account
  • Contact data – email address
  • Workspace data – products, prices, customers, invoices and settings you create
  • Technical data – IP address, browser type, device information for security and performance
  • Usage data – how features are used (aggregated and anonymised where possible)

2. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the PriceLister service
  • Authenticate you via Google OAuth (we never see or store your Google password)
  • Enable collaboration inside your Workspace
  • Improve performance, security and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data security & isolation

Each Workspace is logically isolated. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is controlled by role-based permissions. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Your rights

You may request export or deletion of your Workspace data at any time. Contact support or use the in-app tools available to Creator Admins.
