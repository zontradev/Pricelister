What is PriceLister?

PriceLister is a multi-tenant business workspace. Every company gets its own isolated environment (a Workspace) with products, price lists, customers, invoices, stock levels and role-based team access. Data is stored securely in the cloud and synchronised in real time across devices.

Who is it for?

  • Retail shops and chains that need consistent pricing across locations
  • Distributors and wholesalers managing large catalogues and B2B invoices
  • Growing teams that need clear roles (Creator Admin, Co-Admin, Worker)
  • Businesses that want professional price lists and invoices without complex ERP systems

Explore PriceLister

Deep dive into the modules that power your daily operations.

1. Product Management

Maintain a complete product master. Every product can include:

  • Product name and internal code
  • Size / weight / unit of measure
  • Category and sub-category
  • UPC / barcode
  • Cost price, sale price, base price, MRP
  • Stock quantity and reorder level
  • Product images and variation options

Changes are instantly reflected in price lists and invoices.

2. Price Lists

Create professional price lists filtered by category, customer group or date. Export or print clean layouts suitable for wholesale customers or store displays.

3. Customer Management

Keep a structured customer database with contact details, purchase history and notes. Link invoices and price lists to specific customers for faster order processing.

4. Invoicing

Two invoice modes:

  • Customer Invoices – retail / end-customer focused
  • Business Invoices – B2B with bulk pricing and company details

Both pull live product data so pricing stays accurate.

5. Stock & Inventory

Track quantity on hand, receive stock adjustments, and set low-stock warnings so you never run out of high-velocity items.

6. Team & Permissions

Three built-in roles:

  • Creator Admin – full control, billing, delete workspace
  • Co-Admin – manage products, invoices, workers
  • Worker – limited view and create rights defined by the admin

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At PriceLister we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard information when you use our service.

1. Data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • Identity data – name associated with your Google account
  • Contact data – email address
  • Workspace data – products, prices, customers, invoices and settings you create
  • Technical data – IP address, browser type, device information for security and performance
  • Usage data – how features are used (aggregated and anonymised where possible)

2. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the PriceLister service
  • Authenticate you via Google OAuth (we never see or store your Google password)
  • Enable collaboration inside your Workspace
  • Improve performance, security and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data security & isolation

Each Workspace is logically isolated. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is controlled by role-based permissions. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Your rights

You may request export or deletion of your Workspace data at any time. Contact support or use the in-app tools available to Creator Admins.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions please contact us through the support channels listed in the application.
